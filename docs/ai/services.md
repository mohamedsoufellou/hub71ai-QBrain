# Service context

## Homes
Property prices are AED per year for rent and total AED for purchase. Bedroom filtering means at least the requested count; zero explicitly means a studio minimum. Search accepts annual/total budget, district slug, school proximity ranking, furniture and pets. Strict matches respect every supplied constraint. Distances and photos are illustrative; school distance is not admission or bus eligibility. Landlord and licensed-agent sample offers use the same base property price. Deposits, agency fees, VAT, utilities, cheque count and maintenance need review before tenancy. The original district notes are reference context; generated Wusool Demo communities are fictional.

## Residence and family
Six supported routes: employment, family, golden, green, freelance and investor. Never invent another route as a functioning application path. Search visa scenarios for missing-document, pre-arrival, review, medical and renewal examples. The route catalogue and existing flow contain the relevant checklist and fee illustration. Freelance permission and residence are separate processes. Family sponsorship follows sponsor residence; adult medical fitness applies from age 18. Government approval and medical clearance are not generated by the assistant. Saved applications advance through explicit existing controls.

## Travel
Departure cities and flight IDs come from the catalogue. Fares are illustrative one-way adult amounts. Flights precede hotel and transfer selection. The existing checkout uses a 14-night stay and one traveller's flight; do not imply this is an entire family's quote. Explain additional passenger and room needs separately. Hotel prices are nightly, transfers per example ride. Dates, schedules, baggage, passenger capacity, taxes, child seats and cancellation terms remain unverified.

## Schools
Original real schools have source metadata; additional Demo schools and fees are fictional. Curriculum, correct campus, age/year group and fee exclusions matter. Enquiry controls save a local draft and never reserve a place or contact admissions. Published tuition is an annual amount and may exclude transport, books, uniforms and capital fees.

## Banking
Product descriptions are comparisons, not approved accounts. Bank checks identity, resident status, source of funds and product requirements. Zero demo opening charge does not imply zero monthly or transaction fees. Fictional demo accounts illustrate salary, freelancer, founder, joint, student, nonresident and pension scenarios. Wio's existing control saves an onboarding checklist; other controls show simulated consultation slots.

## Health
Resident plans need emirate, visa status, applicant age, dependant count, income band, employer cover, coverage region and hospital preference. Use the collected form's validated profile. Visitor policies are not covered by the resident plan flow. Never invent hospital network participation, premiums, accepted documents, pre-existing-condition cover or activation. Annual benefit limits are not premiums. Added Demo products are fictional and have no official insurer link. Quote controls save local drafts. Medical visa screening is a separate service.

## Tax and file
Day counts illustrate domestic residence conditions, not comprehensive tax advice or treaty qualification. Salary and business treatment differ. FTA fee examples apply to the stated applicant category; adviser charges are illustrative. File controls show only this session's saved demo receipts, not every seeded visa or tax scenario. Seed scenarios are reference examples and never become the user's history automatically.
